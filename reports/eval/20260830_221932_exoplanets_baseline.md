Here are 5 confirmed exoplanets and the methods used to discover them:

1. **HD 209458 b (Osiris)**
   * **Discovery Method:** Transit Method
   * **Details:** Located about 159 light-years away in the constellation Pegasus, this was the first exoplanet observed transiting its parent star. It was also the first exoplanet to have its atmosphere analyzed.

2. **51 Pegasi b**
   * **Discovery Method:** Radial Velocity Method (Doppler Spectroscopy)
   * **Details:** Located about 50 light-years away, this was the first exoplanet ever discovered orbiting a Sun-like star (in 1995), a breakthrough that earned its discoverers the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2019. It is the archetype for "hot Jupiters."

3. **HR 8799 b**
   * **Discovery Method:** Direct Imaging
   * **Details:** Orbiting a young star roughly 129 light-years away, HR 8799 b is one of several planets in its system that have been directly imaged by telescopes using advanced techniques to block out the blinding glare of the host star.

4. **Kepler-22b**
   * **Discovery Method:** Transit Method
   * **Details:** Discovered by NASA's Kepler Space Telescope, this planet is located about 620 light-years away and was the first known Kepler planet to orbit within the habitable zone of a Sun-like star.

5. **OGLE-2005-BLG-390Lb**
   * **Discovery Method:** Gravitational Microlensing
   * **Details:** Located roughly 21,500 light-years away near the center of our galaxy, this cold, rocky super-Earth was discovered when its gravity acted like a lens, magnifying the light of a background star as it passed in front of it.
