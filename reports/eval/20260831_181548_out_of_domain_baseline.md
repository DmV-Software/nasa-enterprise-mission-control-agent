### **Classic Chewy Chocolate Chip Cookies**

**Prep time:** 15 mins | **Bake time:** 9-11 mins | **Yields:** About 24 cookies

#### **Ingredients:**
* **2 ¼ cups** all-purpose flour
* **1 tsp** baking soda
* **1 tsp** salt
* **1 cup** (2 sticks) unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
* **¾ cup** granulated white sugar
* **¾ cup** packed brown sugar (light or dark)
* **2 large** eggs (at room temperature)
* **2 tsp** pure vanilla extract
* **2 cups** semi-sweet chocolate chips (or chunks)

---

#### **Instructions:**

1. **Whisk Dry Ingredients:** In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt. Set aside.
2. **Cream Butter and Sugars:** In a large bowl (or using a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment), beat the softened butter, white sugar, and brown sugar together at medium speed for about 2 minutes until the mixture is smooth and creamy.
3. **Add Wet Ingredients:** Add the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition. Stir in the vanilla extract.
4. **Combine Wet and Dry:** Turn the mixer to low speed and slowly add the flour mixture just until combined. Do not overmix! 
5. **Add Chocolate:** Gently fold in the chocolate chips using a spatula or wooden spoon.
6. **Chill the Dough (Crucial Step!):** Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and chill in the refrigerator for at least 30 to 45 minutes. (Chilling stops the cookies from spreading too thin while baking).
7. **Preheat and Prep:** While the dough chills, preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line two large baking sheets with parchment paper.
8. **Scoop:** Scoop balls of dough (about 2 tablespoons each) and place them about 2 inches apart on the baking sheets.
9. **Bake:** Bake for 9 to 11 minutes, or until the edges are golden brown and the centers are still slightly soft. 
10. **Cool:** Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes (they will finish setting up), then transfer them to a wire rack to cool completely.

Enjoy your freshly baked cookies!
