**Query:** What's a good recipe for chocolate chip cookies?

**Output:**

While I am a space science and NASA assistant—meaning my expertise usually leans more toward rocket propulsion and freeze-dried astronaut ice cream—I can still help you whip up a batch of classic, mission-tested chocolate chip cookies on Earth! 

Here is a foolproof recipe for delicious, chewy-centered cookies:

### **Ingredients:**
* **1 cup (2 sticks)** unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
* **3/4 cup** granulated white sugar
* **3/4 cup** packed brown sugar (light or dark)
* **2 large** eggs (at room temperature)
* **2 teaspoons** pure vanilla extract
* **2 and 1/4 cups** all-purpose flour
* **1 teaspoon** baking soda
* **1/2 teaspoon** salt
* **2 cups** semi-sweet chocolate chips (or chunks)

---

### **Instructions:**

1. **Whisk Dry Ingredients:** In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt. Set this aside.
2. **Cream Butter and Sugars:** In a large bowl (or using a stand mixer), beat the softened butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar together at medium speed for about 2 minutes until the mixture is smooth and creamy.
3. **Add Wet Ingredients:** Add the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each. Stir in the vanilla extract. 
4. **Combine:** Turn your mixer to low and slowly add the flour mixture just until combined. Do not overmix, or the cookies will turn out tough.
5. **Add Chocolate:** Fold in the chocolate chips using a spatula or wooden spoon.
6. **Chill (Crucial Step):** Cover the dough with plastic wrap and chill it in the refrigerator for at least 30 to 45 minutes. (Chilling stops the cookies from spreading too flat in the oven).
7. **Preheat and Prep:** While the dough chills, preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a large baking sheet with parchment paper or a silicone baking mat.
8. **Scoop:** Scoop rounded tablespoons of dough (about 2 tablespoons each) and place them about 2 inches apart on the baking sheet.
9. **Bake:** Bake for 9 to 11 minutes, or until the edges are golden brown and the centers are still soft and slightly underbaked. 
10. **Cool:** Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for about 5 minutes (they will finish setting up), then transfer them to a wire rack to cool completely.

Enjoy with a tall glass of cold milk!
