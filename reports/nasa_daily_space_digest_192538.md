#### 1. Astronomy Picture of the Day (APOD)
* **Title:** M74: A Grand Design Spiral
* **Image URL:** [M74 Spiral Galaxy (960px)](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/image/2608/m74_hst_960.jpg) | [HD Version](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/image/2608/m74_hst_1280.jpg)
* **Scientific Summary:** 
  Located approximately 32 million light-years away in the constellation Pisces, Messier 74 (M74) is a classic example of a "grand design" spiral galaxy. This face-on island universe contains an estimated 100 billion stars and spans roughly 30,000 light-years across. This sharp composite image, constructed from Hubble Space Telescope archival data, highlights the galaxy's graceful spiral arms traced by bright blue star clusters and dark cosmic dust lanes. Reddish glows scattered throughout the arms indicate large star-forming regions rich in active hydrogen emission.
