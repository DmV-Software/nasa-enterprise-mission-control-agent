### 1. Astronomy Picture of the Day (APOD)
* **Date:** August 30, 2026
* **Title:** M74: A Grand Design Spiral
* **Image URL:** [High-Resolution (1280px)](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/image/2608/m74_hst_1280.jpg) | [Standard (960px)](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/image/2608/m74_hst_960.jpg)
* **Scientific Summary:** 
  M74 is a classic "grand design" spiral galaxy located approximately 32 million light-years away in the constellation Pisces. This face-on island universe contains an estimated 100 billion stars and spans roughly 30,000 light-years across. This sharp composite image, constructed from Hubble Space Telescope archival data, highlights its graceful spiral arms traced by bright blue star clusters and dark cosmic dust lanes. Reddish glows within the spiral arms indicate large star-forming regions rich in hydrogen gas emissions.
