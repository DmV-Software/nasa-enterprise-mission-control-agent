### NASA Astronomy Picture of the Day (APOD) — 2026-08-30

**Title:** M74: A Grand Design Spiral
**Date:** August 30, 2026

#### Scientific Overview & Explanation
Messier 74 (M74) is widely regarded as one of the most photogenic face-on spiral galaxies in our night sky. Located approximately 32 million light-years away in the constellation Pisces, this "island universe" contains an estimated 100 billion stars. 

Classified as an Sc-type spiral galaxy, M74 is a textbook example of a "grand design" spiral. Its prominent, graceful spiral arms are clearly defined and beautifully traced by clusters of young, hot blue stars and dark, obscuring lanes of cosmic dust. 

This high-resolution composite image was constructed using archival data from the Hubble Space Telescope. Spanning roughly 30,000 light-years across, the image incorporates narrow-band exposures that capture the characteristic reddish glow of ionized hydrogen gas (H-alpha emissions), highlighting the galaxy's active, massive star-forming regions (H II regions).
